# WTC_Twin_Towers
![World Trade Center 3D Project Logo](docs/images/WTC_Project_Logo.png)
A Three.js Recreation of a picture of the Original World Trade Center in New York City. Intended as a 9/11 Tribute.
# Software Requirements
You only need Servez with its path set to wherever you've downloaded the repository.
# Introduction
## The World Trade Center
From 1973 until their destruction on September 11th, 2001, the Twin Towers of the World Trade Center were the tallest buildings in New York City. Built by the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey and designed by architect Minoru Yamasaki, each tower had 21,800 windows and roughly an acre of space for each of its 110 floors. The North Tower stood at 1,368 ft tall, with an additional 360 ft for the broadcasting mast, while the South Tower was 1,362 ft tall and had both an indoor and outdoor observation deck. Standing right at the footprints of the two towers were a 22-story hotel, four additional office buildings, and an underground shopping mall. The Twin Towers formed a distinct part of the Lower Manhattan skyline and quickly became a New York icon, appearing in countless TV shows and movies.
## Purpose
This project is an interactive 1:100 scale replica of the original World Trade Center. You can explore the complex and its surrounding buildings, and see the Twin Towers from different angles.
### Controls
- WASD Keys: Move forward, backward, left, and right
- Q and E Keys: Roll you clockwise and counter-clockwise
- R and F Keys: Pan up and down
- Arrow Keys: Look up, down, left, and right
## Scope
I gave myself a month to work on this project, and in that timespan I replicated the following buildings you see in the picture above:
- The Twin Towers (WTC 1 and 2)
- The Marriott WTC Hotel
- WTC 4, 5, 6 (The black office buildings adjacent to the towers)
- WTC 7 (The Trapezoidal Red Building)
- Millennium Downtown NYC (The black glass building standing in the background between the two towers)
- One Liberty Plaza (A slightly wider black tower located to the right of the picture)
- Brooklyn Place (World Financial Center) Towers 1 and 2 (Respectively with a trapezoid and dome-shaped roof)
- Part of the Winter Garden Atrium
- 395 South End Avenue (In the foreground on the far-right of the picture)
